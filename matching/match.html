How to Play

1. Objective

Match each preferred image with another corresponding preferred image.

2. Selection

Click one image on the left, then click the image on the right you think pairs with it, or vice versa. Correct matches fade out and reappear after some time.

3. Scoring

Correct matches increase your score and streak. Incorrect guesses may reset streaks.
