How to Play

1. Objective

Match each synthesized (left) image with its corresponding natural-preferred (right) image.

2. Selection

Click one image on the left, then click the image on the right you think pairs with it. Correct matches lock in.

3. Scoring

Correct matches increase your score and streak. Incorrect guesses may reset streaks.

4. Tips

  • Green: Correct pair locked.
  • Yellow: Near match (visual similarity).
  • Red: Incorrect pairing.
